About Lernoviq

Built on Experience. Driven by Innovation. Designed for the Future of Learning.

Lernoviq is an emerging learning technology and digital education solutions brand established in 2026. Its vision is to make organizational learning more intelligent, adaptable and effective through a connected approach to content, platforms and delivery.

Our story

Solutions built around the organization, its learners and its goals.

The brand is founded on more than 26 years of professional leadership experience across enterprise technology, learning programs, instructional design, digital content and complex project execution. That experience informs how we approach the full learning lifecycle: understanding needs, shaping curricula, collaborating with subject matter experts, developing courses, validating outcomes and supporting delivery.

Rather than fitting every client into the same model, we aim to build solutions around the organization, its learners and its operational goals. Through our technology partnership with Innogenx and the ongoing development of Lernoviq LMS, we are building an integrated approach that connects learning expertise with modern software capabilities.

Our mission

To help organizations worldwide create meaningful, accessible and measurable learning experiences by combining expert-led learning design, custom digital content, adaptable technology and accountable delivery.

Our vision

To become a trusted global partner for intelligent learning solutions — making it simpler for organizations to develop people, strengthen capabilities and adapt to change.
