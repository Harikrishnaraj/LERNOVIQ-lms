LERNOVIQ LMS

Learning Technology Designed Around Your Organization.

LERNOVIQ LMS is being developed to support a connected digital learning experience, pairing learning-domain expertise with an adaptable technology foundation.

Product philosophy

A good learning platform should enable the learning strategy — not dictate it. We understand how an organization creates content, delivers training, engages learners and reviews results, then align platform implementation with those workflows.

Where it is headed

Our product direction focuses on the learning functions organizations commonly need: managing content, structuring learning experiences, supporting knowledge assessment, providing visibility into learning activity and connecting learning operations. Specific capabilities, integrations, subscription tiers and availability are confirmed during product discovery and demonstration.

Who it is for

  • Enterprises exploring an adaptable platform for employee learning.
  • BFSI organizations evaluating structured curricula and assessment needs.
  • Government or institutional teams planning scalable digital training.
  • Training providers seeking a tailored learning delivery environment.
Technology foundation

The documented deployment design uses a Next.js application, container-based deployment with Docker, Caddy-managed HTTPS and a GitHub Actions release workflow. It provides for a separate content origin to support multi-frame SCORM packages, scheduled reporting jobs and Supabase authentication configuration. These are architectural provisions documented in the deployment guide, not independently verified end-user features.
